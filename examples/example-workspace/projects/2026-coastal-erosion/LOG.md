# Log — Coastal erosion under compound storm surge
_Append-only. Newest entries at the top. 2–4 lines per entry: what happened,
what was decided, why. Never rewrite an old entry._

## 2026-07-21
Rewrote the results section for the joint model. Retreat estimates on the
north-facing stretches are 22% higher than under the independent treatment,
which is the paper's actual contribution and was buried before. Discussion
still describes the old results and has to follow.

## 2026-06-30
Switched from treating surge and rainfall as independent to a joint copula
model. Supersedes the 2026-03-12 decision to keep them independent. The
independence assumption was chosen for tractability, but the Sandvik data
shows the interaction is exactly where the interesting behaviour is, so
tractability was buying us the wrong paper.

## 2026-04-18
Agreed authorship order with Mira and Tomas: Halvorsen, Lindqvist, Reidar.
Mira owns the modelling section and the sensitivity analysis, Tomas the data
description. Recorded here because it was agreed in a corridor conversation
and exists nowhere else.

## 2026-03-12
Target venue set to Journal of Coastal Systems rather than the NordCoast
conference proceedings. Slower, but the audience for a methods contribution
is there and the page limit at NordCoast would have cut the sensitivity
analysis.

## 2026-02-10
Project started. Three seasons of Sandvik field data available, consortium
work package 3 covers the time.
