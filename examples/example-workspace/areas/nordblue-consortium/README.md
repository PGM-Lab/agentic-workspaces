# NORDBLUE consortium

**What:** work package 3 lead in the NORDBLUE coastal resilience consortium
**Since:** 2025-11-03
**Cadence:** monthly partner call, first Tuesday. Annual report due each January.
**Key people:** the consortium coordinator (Fjordvik Institute), WP2 lead Mira Lindqvist

Standing responsibility for as long as the consortium runs (through 2028).
Covers the partner calls, the reporting, and the data-sharing agreement.
Papers produced under the consortium are separate units in `projects/` with a
pointer back here; they are not nested inside this folder.

An area has no completion state. When NORDBLUE ends in 2028 this moves to
`archive/`.

## Where things are

- `notes/` — partner call notes, one file per call, dated
- `agreement/` — the consortium agreement and the data-sharing annex

The data-sharing annex is the document that governs what partner data may be
sent to external services. Read it before any such decision.
